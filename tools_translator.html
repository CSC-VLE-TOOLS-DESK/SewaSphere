Please note: Hindi and Punjabi come from free online translation services (MyMemory, with Google Translate as backup) and your text is sent to them. Haryanvi and Bhojpuri are not offered by normal translators, so the Free method turns Hindi into the dialect by changing common words. It is only a help and may miss local style, so please read it before using it. The AI method sends your text and your own key straight to OpenAI and usually gives better dialect text. For official papers, ask a person who knows the language to check.

About Translator
