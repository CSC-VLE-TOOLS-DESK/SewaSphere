About Ledger & Khata Books

Bahi Khata and ledger books for kirana, medical, sweet, cloth, garment and book shops, insurance agents, share trading and your home accounts. All tools run inside your browser, so your photos and documents are not uploaded to a server. They are free to use and need no sign-up.

Every ledger keeps its data on your own device. Use the Backup button inside the ledger to save a file, and Restore to load it again.
