Frequently Asked Questions

What is SevaSphere – CSC VLE Tools?

SevaSphere is an independent collection of online tools for CSC VLEs, digital service centres, cyber cafes, students and everyday users. It includes photo and signature tools, PDF utilities, document formats, ID card layouts, business ledgers and calculators.

Are the tools free to use?

The tools are provided for free. Some features may require an internet connection or third-party browser libraries.

Are these tools an official government website?

No. SevaSphere is an independent project and is not affiliated with CSC e-Governance Services India Ltd, UIDAI or any government department.
