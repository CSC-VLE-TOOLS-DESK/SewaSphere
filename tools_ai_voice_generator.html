Hindi, English and Hinglish are supported. In Hinglish mode, Devanagari and English words are read with matching voices. Voice names and accents may differ between computers.

⬇ Download audio
  1. Click Record.
  2. In the pop-up choose This tab and turn on Share tab audio.
  3. Wait until the voice finishes, then download.
Privacy: Free mode keeps your script on this device. AI voice mode sends your script and your key directly to OpenAI (not to any other server). It uses your OpenAI credit.
For best results, use an updated desktop browser and make sure Hindi/English speech voices are available in your computer's language settings.
