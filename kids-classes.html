About Kids Learning Classes 2 to 5

Practice for Class 2, 3, 4 and 5 children: a short lesson and a quiz in Maths, English, Hindi, EVS and General Knowledge. Maths questions change every time, so children can practise again and again. It is a free online tool on SevaSphere, made for families, schools and CSC centres.

  • Runs in your browser, so your files are not uploaded to our servers
  • Works on mobile, tablet and desktop
